How it works

Idea Sorter turns a messy thought into a plan you can act on.

1 · Capture

Type the idea

Write it how you'd say it. Mention a time like "Friday", "next week" or "someday" if you have one.

2 · Sort

It gets a when, why and how

Claude picks a date, writes why it matters and 2–5 steps, and sets a category, priority and size. You can change any of it.

3 · Act

Make it happen

From an idea, add it to your Outlook calendar, draft a Gmail email to someone who can help, or check website names on GoDaddy.

4 · Finish

Tick off steps

Check steps as you go and mark the idea done. Overdue ideas float to the top so nothing slips.

Your ideas are private to your claude.ai account and sync between your devices.
