    House Rules

    • 4 armies share one connected cross-shaped board.
    • Turn order: Red → Blue → Yellow → Green.
    • No "check" — capture a king to eliminate that player, their remaining pieces are removed.
    • Last player standing wins.
    • Pawns auto-promote to Queen at the board's far edge.
