Lumo Firefly

Lumo rarity

The Lumo Firefly is the rarest pet in Lumo Study, the free study game where your notes, a photo of your textbook or any topic become quiz games you play until you truly understand. It is Lumo rarity, above Galaxial: nobody can hatch it from an egg or upgrade to it. Only the creator of Lumo can give it.

It hovers on buzzing glassy wings, its gold lantern pulses, and it loops in the air leaving a trail of gold. On your team it has Lumo power: +75% XP in all (its own +25% plus Lumo power), +50% coins on everything you earn, more hearts and eggs that hatch 50% faster.

Study with Lumo, free

How studying with Lumo works

  1. Snap your notes or a page of your textbook, or just type a topic.
  2. Lumo writes real test questions about it.
  3. Play them in game modes (Challenge, speed run, boss battle) or in Live games with friends, until you get it.
  4. Every answer warms your egg: hatch pets, level them up and build a team of 4.

Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythical, Exotic, Galaxial and, at the very top, Lumo.
